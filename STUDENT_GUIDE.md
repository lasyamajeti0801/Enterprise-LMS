# Enterprise Learning Management System - Student Guide

Welcome to your student learning hub! This guide outlines how to enroll in courses, study lessons, take timed quizzes, submit assignments, track grades, and download official PDF certificates.

---

## 🔑 Accessing the Student Portal

1. Navigate to the landing page and click **Student Portal** (or visit `/auth/login?portal=student`).
2. Log in with your student credentials:
   - **Sample User**: `student_alex` / `student_sarah`
   - **Password**: `studentpassword123`
   *(New students can click "Register Account" to create a new profile).*
3. You will land on your **Student Home Dashboard** (`/student/dashboard`).

---

## 📚 1. Course Enrollment & Catalog

1. Click **Course Catalog** (`/student/catalog`).
2. Browse accredited courses across Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Cybersecurity.
3. Click **Enroll Now** to instantly join a course.

---

## 🖥️ 2. The Interactive Learning Player

1. Navigate to **My Courses** &rarr; select an enrolled course &rarr; click **Continue Learning** (`/student/learn/<course_id>`).
2. The split-view learning interface features:
   - **Left Sidebar**: Complete curriculum outline, module progression, and completion indicators.
   - **Main Content Area**: Rich lesson texts, syntax-highlighted code blocks, and embedded resources.
   - **Action Bar**: Click **Mark as Completed** to advance your progress.

---

## ⏱️ 3. Taking Quizzes

1. Select a quiz from the course curriculum or student dashboard.
2. Click **Start Quiz** to launch the interactive quiz runner:
   - A live countdown timer tracks remaining time.
   - Select your answers for each question.
   - Click **Submit Quiz** (or let the timer auto-submit when time expires).
3. Review your instant score, percentage, passing status, and detailed question explanations on the results page.

---

## 📝 4. Submitting Assignments

1. Navigate to **Assignments** (`/student/dashboard` or course page).
2. Click on the active assignment to view instructions and due date.
3. Enter your written response/solution code in the submission editor.
4. Click **Submit Assignment**. Once reviewed by the instructor, your score and feedback will appear in your gradebook.

---

## 🏆 5. Grades, GPA & Certificates

* **My Grades** (`/student/grades`): View your complete academic transcript, GPA, and course marks.
* **Certificate Download Center** (`/student/certificates`): Upon completing all lessons and passing required assessments in a course, your official vector PDF certificate is generated automatically. Click **Download PDF** to save your high-resolution credential.
* **Credential Verification**: Share your unique certificate verification code (or URL) with employers, who can verify its authenticity at `/verify`.

---

## 🎮 6. Gamification, Badges & Streaks

* Visit the **Gamification Hub** (`/student/gamification`) to track:
  - **XP & Level Progress**: Earn points by completing lessons and scoring well on quizzes.
  - **Badges Earned**: Unlock achievements like *Quiz Ace*, *Code Architect*, and *First Step*.
  - **Learning Streaks**: Maintain consecutive daily study streaks.
