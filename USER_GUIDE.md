# Enterprise Learning Management System - General User Guide

Welcome to the Enterprise Learning Management System (LMS). This guide provides an overview of the platform architecture, navigation, account setup, and common features.

---

## 🏛️ System Overview

The LMS is an all-in-one educational platform engineered for students, educators, and institutional administrators. It provides:
1. **Three Role Portals**: Tailored user experiences for Administrators, Instructors, and Students.
2. **Interactive Learning**: Markdown & video lessons, interactive quizzes, assignments, and study materials.
3. **Automated Credentials**: High-resolution vector PDF certificates upon course completion.
4. **Gamified Motivation**: XP levels, badges, and learning streaks.
5. **Community & Collaboration**: Course discussion forums, instructor Q&A, and academic calendar.

---

## 🚪 Logging In & Portal Selection

1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:5000/`.
2. The landing page presents three distinct cards:
   * **Admin Portal**: Institutional governance, system configuration, audit logs, and reports.
   * **Instructor Portal**: Course creation, curriculum design, quiz builder, and gradebook.
   * **Student Portal**: Course enrollment, interactive learning player, quizzes, and certificates.
3. Click the card corresponding to your institutional role to proceed to the dedicated login portal.

---

## 🌓 Themes & Display Customization

* **Dark / Light Mode**: Use the theme toggle in the top-right navigation bar to switch between modern Dark and Light aesthetics. Your preference is persisted across browser sessions.
* **Responsive Layout**: The LMS layout adapts fluidly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop workstations.

---

## 🔔 Notifications & Calendar

* **Notification Inbox** (`/notifications`): Alerts regarding assignment deadlines, quiz evaluations, grade publications, and system announcements.
* **Academic Calendar** (`/calendar`): Interactive schedule displaying upcoming classes, assignment due dates, and exam windows.
